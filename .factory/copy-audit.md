## Labels and headings checked

The non-sentence labels are direct names for the task or control: Interactive
filter and EQ listener; Listening lab; Compare filter settings; Frequency
response; Choose a filter type; Sample settings; Start with a sample setting;
Filter basics; How it works; Privacy and limits; and Keep the lesson local.
They name a section, state, or action without a mood heading.
